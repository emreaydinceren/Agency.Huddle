# Relocation basics

## When it applies

- One move, from the first housing search to the first week in the new place.
- The team searches, compares and tracks. The Human views, signs, books and pays.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task names the search, comparison or change and has an owner. Done when the move coordinator assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the housing scout, comparison shopper, inbox triager or schedule keeper gathers listings, quotes, mail or dates with sources. Done when they are in the Task.
3. **Verify**: the move coordinator checks the result against the checklist and the must-haves and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line.

## Who does what

- `move-coordinator` keeps the dated move checklist, checks each result and posts the Evidence line.
- `housing-scout` searches listings and keeps a cited shortlist.
- `comparison-shopper` compares movers, utilities and other quotes on the same terms.
- `inbox-triager` sorts move-related mail and lists what needs action.
- `schedule-keeper` keeps the move calendar and flags clashes.

## If a role is not hired

- No `housing-scout`: the Human sends listings, and the move coordinator checks them against the must-haves.
- No `comparison-shopper`: the move coordinator lists the quotes as they arrive.
- No `inbox-triager`: the Human forwards move mail to the team.
- No `schedule-keeper`: the move coordinator keeps the dates in the checklist.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The move coordinator's Evidence line is the check, and the Human signs and pays.

## Cadence

- Work back from the move date. Notice periods and bookings come first.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- Leases, deposits, bookings and anything that costs money go to the Human.
- A listing that looks like a scam goes to the Human at once.

## Onboarding

- Note the move date, the old and new areas, and who is moving.
- Write Team Memory notes on the must-haves, the budget and the accounts that need a new address.
- Propose a starter Task, such as the move checklist.
