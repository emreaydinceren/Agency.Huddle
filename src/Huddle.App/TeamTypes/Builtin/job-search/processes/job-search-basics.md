# Job search basics

## Identity

This Team runs an active job search: finding openings, tailoring documents, preparing for interviews and following up. Work that arrives here is one opening or goal at a time, each with its acceptance in one line. Negotiating an offer or answering a salary question goes to the Human, and learning a new skill belongs to the learning Team type.

## When it applies

- An active job search: finding openings, tailoring documents, preparing for interviews and following up.
- The Task states its acceptance in one line, so the Career Coach can check it without a meeting.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task names the opening or goal and states its acceptance in one line. Done when the Career Coach assigns it.
2. **In progress**: an agent drafts the short list, the resume, the cover letter, the practice questions or the follow-up. Done when the draft is in the Task.
3. **Verify**: the Career Coach checks the draft against the posting and the Human's own facts and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human submits, sends or practises and marks the Task Done.

## Who does what

- `career-coach` agrees the goals, writes acceptance lines, checks each draft and posts the Evidence line.
- `job-scout` finds and summarises openings that fit.
- `resume-writer` tailors the resume and drafts cover letters from the Human's facts only.
- `interview-coach` runs practice interviews and gives feedback.
- `application-tracker` keeps the tracker and drafts follow-ups.

## If a role is not hired

- Any missing role: the Career Coach does that work itself and says so on the Task.
- The manager always verifies, so a Task with no draft goes to the Human with the posting quoted.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The Career Coach's Evidence line is the check, and the Human closes each Task.

## Definition of Done

- Claims checked: evidence from verify
- Application reviewed: document reviewed by verify

## Cadence

- Work the open Tasks by closing date, nearest first.
- The Career Coach posts a weekly review of what was sent and what is pending.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- An offer, a salary question, or anything that commits the Human goes to the Human.
- A disagreement between agents goes to the Human with both views written on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Note the Job search folder, the current resume and the target roles, places and pay.
- Write Team Memory notes on the Human's strengths, deal-breakers and writing voice.
- Suggest a starter Task.
