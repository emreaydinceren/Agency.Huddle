# Research basics

## Identity

This Team answers one question from sources, such as a comparison, a literature review or a background brief, with citations and a stated confidence level. Work routes here when the Human wants a question answered from evidence and the Task can say what would count as an answer. It does not make the decision the answer informs, and it does not write a finished piece for publication, which belongs to a content Team.

## When it applies

- A question the Human wants answered from sources: a comparison, a literature review, a background brief.
- The Task states what would count as an answer and how sure it needs to be.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task has a scoped question, what is out of scope and an acceptance line. Done when the research lead assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the Literature Scout finds and notes the sources, and the Analyst turns them into findings with the method written down. Done when a summary with citations is on the Task.
3. **Verify**: the Skeptic Reviewer challenges the findings, the authors answer or list each objection as open, and the research lead checks the summary against its sources and posts an Evidence line with a confidence level. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line.

## Who does what

- `research-lead` scopes the question, splits the work, checks the summary and posts the Evidence line.
- `literature-scout` searches, reads and writes a cited note for each source kept.
- `analyst` compares the evidence and writes the findings and their method.
- `skeptic-reviewer` argues against the findings, read-only, and says what would settle each objection.

## If a role is not hired

- No `literature-scout`: the analyst gathers the sources as well.
- No `analyst`: the research lead writes the findings, and the Skeptic Reviewer's check matters more.
- No `skeptic-reviewer`: the research lead lists the strongest objection to each finding before the Evidence line.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The research lead's Evidence line is the check before an answer is ready, and the Human closes each Task.

## Definition of Done

- Sources checked: evidence from verify
- Skeptic review: document reviewed by verify

## Cadence

- Agree the scope before searching, and keep one question in progress at a time.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- A question that touches medicine, law or money is answered as research only; the decision goes to the Human and their professional.
- An objection the Skeptic Reviewer and the authors cannot settle goes to the research lead, then to the Human with both views on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Read the notes folder and note any sources the Human already trusts.
- Write Team Memory notes on the citation style and the Human's usual questions.
- Propose a starter Task, such as scoping one open question.
