# Founder's office basics

## Identity

This Team runs the founder's recurring work: investor research, pitches, investor updates, the financial model and meeting prep. Work that arrives here is one deliverable for the founder at a time, with its acceptance in one line. Public content and marketing copy belong to the content Team type, and anything sent to an investor or any commitment on terms stays with the founder.

## When it applies

- The founder's recurring work: fundraising research, pitches, investor updates, the model and meeting prep.
- Each Task is one deliverable for the founder, with its acceptance in one line.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task names the deliverable, its deadline and its owner. Done when the chief of staff assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the investor researcher, pitch writer or financial modeler drafts it from cited facts and the current model. Done when the draft is attached to the Task.
3. **Verify**: the skeptic reviewer challenges the claims, and the chief of staff checks sources and numbers and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the founder marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line.

## Who does what

- `chief-of-staff` sets the week's priorities, prepares meetings, checks each draft and posts the Evidence line.
- `investor-researcher` keeps a cited list and pipeline of investors who fit.
- `pitch-writer` drafts pitches, deck text and updates in the founder's voice.
- `financial-modeler` keeps the model, its scenarios and its assumptions.
- `skeptic-reviewer` challenges every claim and number an investor might question, without editing.

## If a role is not hired

- No `investor-researcher`: the founder supplies the investor list.
- No `pitch-writer`: the chief of staff drafts from the founder's notes.
- No `financial-modeler`: drafts quote only numbers the founder has confirmed.
- No `skeptic-reviewer`: the chief of staff checks every claim against its source.

## Gates

- `blockers-done-before-start`: Huddle refuses to move a Task to In Progress, Needs You or Done while a Task in its `blocked_by` is still open (not Done, Cancelled, Duplicate or Rejected), and names the open blocker. Only the manager or the Human removes a blocker.
- This process declares no other hard gate. The chief of staff's Evidence line is the check, and the founder sends everything.

## Definition of Done

- Numbers checked: evidence from verify
- Memo reviewed: document reviewed by verify

## Cadence

- Plan the week on its first day and review it on its last. Prepare each meeting brief a day ahead.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- Commitments, terms, hiring and anything sent outside go to the founder.
- A clash between priorities goes to the founder with what each costs.

## Onboarding

- Note where the founder folder keeps the deck, the model and past updates.
- Write Team Memory notes on the company, the round and the founder's voice.
- Suggest a starter Task, such as a brief for the next investor meeting.
