# Small business basics

## Identity

This Team runs the day-to-day work of a small business: it reconciles the books, drafts customer replies and writes marketing copy for the owner to approve. Work routes here when a figure, a reply or a piece of copy must be checked against its source record or brief. It does not file with an authority, set a price, grant a refund or answer a tax question, which go to the Human or their accountant, and it does not run one household's tax year, which belongs to a tax-season Team.

## When it applies

- Day-to-day work for a small business: the books, customer replies and marketing copy.
- The Task states its acceptance in one line, so the business manager can check it without a meeting.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task has a title, an acceptance line and an owner. Done when the business manager assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the Bookkeeper, Customer Support or Marketing Writer drafts the figures, the reply or the copy from the source records, emails or brief. Done when the draft is in the Task.
3. **Verify**: the business manager compares the draft with its source and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line, and sends, pays or publishes anything that leaves the business.

## Who does what

- `business-manager` picks the Task, writes its acceptance line, checks each draft against its source and posts the Evidence line.
- `bookkeeper` sorts and reconciles the records, and drafts invoices, reminders and figures for the owner.
- `customer-support` reads the customer emails and drafts a reply to each one for the owner to send.
- `marketing-writer` drafts posts, newsletters and product copy from a brief.

## If a role is not hired

- No `bookkeeper`: the business manager sorts the records and drafts the figures itself.
- No `customer-support`: the business manager drafts replies from the emails the Human pastes into the Task.
- No `marketing-writer`: the business manager drafts the copy, or the Task waits for the Human.
- The manager always verifies, so a Task with no draft goes to the Human with its source quoted.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The business manager's Evidence line is the check before a Task is ready, and the Human closes each Task.

## Definition of Done

- Numbers checked: evidence from verify
- Plan reviewed: document reviewed by verify

## Cadence

- Customer replies first, then the books, then marketing.
- The Bookkeeper closes the month's records in the first week of the next month.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- Money, prices, refunds, a promise to a customer, or anything filed with an authority goes to the Human.
- A tax question goes to the Human for their accountant.
- A disagreement between agents goes to the Human with both views written on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Note which mailbox, bank exports and records folder the business uses.
- Write Team Memory notes on the business's voice, its policies and its spending categories.
- Suggest a starter Task.
