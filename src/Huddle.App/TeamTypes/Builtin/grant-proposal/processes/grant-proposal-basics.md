# Grant proposal basics

## When it applies

- One proposal to one funder's call, from choosing the call to a draft the Human can submit.
- Each Task is one section, budget or attachment, with the call's requirement as its acceptance line.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task names the section, quotes the call's requirement and has an owner. Done when the proposal lead assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the funder researcher, grant writer or budget analyst drafts the section from cited facts. Done when the draft is in the Task.
3. **Verify**: the compliance checker reports gaps against the call, and the proposal lead checks the draft and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line.

## Who does what

- `proposal-lead` breaks the call into Tasks, checks each draft against it and posts the Evidence line.
- `funder-researcher` finds and confirms funders and calls, with eligibility and deadlines cited.
- `grant-writer` drafts the narrative sections to the funder's questions and limits.
- `budget-analyst` builds the budget and its justification from the Human's costs.
- `compliance-checker` reads each draft against the call and lists every gap, without editing.

## If a role is not hired

- No `funder-researcher`: the Human chooses the call and the proposal lead confirms its rules from the funder's page.
- No `grant-writer`: the proposal lead drafts the narrative.
- No `budget-analyst`: the proposal lead drafts the budget and marks every figure as an assumption.
- No `compliance-checker`: the proposal lead checks the call's rules itself before the Evidence line.

## Gates

- `qa-evidence-before-done`: Huddle refuses to move a Task to Done until a member whose role covers verify posts an Evidence line.

## Cadence

- Work back from the funder's deadline, and leave the last three days for checking.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- Eligibility doubts, money, partners and anything the Human must sign go to the Human.
- A disagreement between the writer and the compliance checker goes to the proposal lead, then to the Human with both views on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Note where the proposals folder keeps calls, drafts and past proposals.
- Write Team Memory notes on the organisation's mission, past awards and voice.
- Propose a starter Task, such as listing the requirements of the next call.
