# Content basics

## Identity

This Team takes an article, post or newsletter from a brief to a finished draft that the Human publishes. Each piece is written against its brief for audience, angle, length and deadline, and every fact is checked against a source before the editor-in-chief signs it off. Documentation that lives in a repository belongs to the `docs-team` Team type, and the Team never publishes a piece itself.

## Editorial Rules

Follow the voice principles in `Teams/AGENTS.md`. Write to the audience and angle the brief names, and put the source beside every figure, quote and claim it makes.

## When it applies

- Articles, posts, newsletters and other pieces that go from an idea to a finished draft the Human publishes.
- Each Task carries a brief: the audience, the angle, the length and the deadline.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task has a brief, an acceptance line and a Writer. Done when the editor-in-chief assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the Writer outlines and drafts the piece in the content folder, with a source beside every fact. Done when the draft is linked on the Task.
3. **Verify**: the Fact-Checker reports on every claim, the Copy Editor proposes line edits, and the Writer resolves both. The editor-in-chief reads the result against the brief and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the Human marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line, and publishes the piece.

## Who does what

- `editor-in-chief` writes the brief, assigns the draft, makes the final read and posts the Evidence line.
- `writer` outlines, drafts and revises the piece against the notes.
- `fact-checker` checks each fact, figure, quote and source and reports, read-only.
- `copy-editor` proposes fixes for grammar, clarity, consistency and house style as suggestions.

## If a role is not hired

- No `fact-checker`: the editor-in-chief checks every figure and quote against its source before the Evidence line.
- No `copy-editor`: the editor-in-chief does the line pass.
- The `writer` is required; the editor-in-chief does not draft and verify the same piece.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The editor-in-chief's Evidence line is the check before a piece is ready, and the Human closes each Task.

## Definition of Done

- Edit pass: document reviewed by verify
- Claims checked: evidence from verify

## Cadence

- Keep no more than two pieces in progress per Writer.
- Fact-check before the copy edit, so line edits are not wasted on text that will change.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- A claim about a real person, a legal risk or a change of angle goes to the Human.
- A disagreement between the Writer and an editor goes to the editor-in-chief, then to the Human with both views on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Read the content folder and note its layout and any published examples.
- Write Team Memory notes on the house style, the voice and the usual audiences.
- Suggest a starter Task, such as a brief for one piece the Human has asked about.
