# Book project basics

## When it applies

- One manuscript, worked chapter by chapter, from outline to a draft the author is happy with.
- The author owns the book. The team drafts what the author asks for and reports on the rest.

## Stages

1. **Ready**: the Task names the chapter and what the work on it must achieve. Done when the developmental editor assigns it.
2. **In progress**: the writer drafts or revises the chapter. Done when the draft is in the manuscript folder and linked from the Task.
3. **Verify**: the continuity checker, beta reader and copy editor report, and the developmental editor checks the chapter against the notes and posts an Evidence line. Done when that line exists.
4. **Done**: the author marks the Task Done after reading the Evidence line.

## Who does what

- `developmental-editor` keeps the chapter map, orders the notes and posts the Evidence line.
- `writer` drafts and revises chapters in the author's voice.
- `continuity-checker` keeps the story bible and flags contradictions, without editing.
- `beta-reader` reports a reader's reactions, without editing.
- `copy-editor` reports grammar, spelling and style against the style sheet.

## If a role is not hired

- No `writer`: the author writes, and the team only reviews.
- No `continuity-checker`: the developmental editor keeps the story bible.
- No `beta-reader`: the developmental editor notes where pacing drops.
- No `copy-editor`: copy editing waits until the structure is settled.

## Gates

- This process declares no hard gate. The developmental editor's Evidence line is the check, and the author decides every change.

## Definition of Done

- Editor review: document reviewed by verify
- Consistency check: evidence from verify

## Cadence

- Structure before sentences: copy edits wait until a chapter's structure is settled.
- A retro runs every 14 days.

## Escalation

- Changes to the premise, the ending or a main character go to the author.
- Conflicting notes go to the author with both written on the Task.

## Onboarding

- Note how the manuscript folder is laid out by chapter.
- Write Team Memory notes on the genre, the reader, the author's voice and the style sheet.
- Propose a starter Task, such as a chapter map of the current draft.
