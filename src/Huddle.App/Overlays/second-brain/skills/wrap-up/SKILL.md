# Wrap up a session

This Skill captures what the session taught the Team before it is forgotten. It
proposes first and writes only what the Human approves.

## Steps

1. Look back over the session: what was decided, what went wrong, what was made
   and what is still open.
2. Propose, grouped by destination:
   - **Rules** for `AGENTS.md`, only for how the Team should always work.
   - **Memory** entries for facts that will matter again.
   - **Outputs** for finished work worth keeping, each with a description.
   - **Tasks** for what is still open.
3. Keep each proposal to a line or two, and say what in the session it came from.
4. Show the whole list and wait for the Human's OK. Nothing is written yet.
5. Write only what the Human approved, each to its destination. Leave out what
   they declined.
6. Tell the Human what you wrote and where.
