# Harbor Lane Bakery: how we run

Written by the owner, Sam Rivera. Everything here is fictional test data.

## The business

- Retail shop open **Tuesday to Sunday, 07:00 to 15:00**. Closed on Monday.
- Wholesale deliveries go out on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday mornings.
- Business mailbox: hello@harborlane.example.com.
- Records live in the shared folder "Harbor Lane Books". Bank exports are monthly CSV files.

## Our voice

Warm and plain. Short sentences. No exclamation marks. Sign replies "Harbor Lane Bakery".

## Policies

- **Retail complaints:** staff may offer a replacement or a credit of up to $25 the same day.
- **Refunds of any size on a wholesale order are the owner's decision.** Never promise one.
- **Payment terms:** wholesale invoices are due 14 days after issue.
- **Prices** are set by the owner and listed on the wholesale price sheet.

## Spending categories

Ingredients, Packaging, Utilities, Wages, Marketing.

## Customers worth knowing

- Morning Tide Hotel is our largest wholesale account.
- Tidewater Cafe has ordered every week since 2024.
